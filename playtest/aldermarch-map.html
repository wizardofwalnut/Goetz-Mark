Design intent

Ironthroat is the only link between the two basins — removing it splits the map 9/9. Stone is scarce (3 of 19) and never in a starting county, so every player must expand or trade for castle upgrades. The four seats are structurally identical: same neighbour count, same terrain in early reach, same road distance to the pass. That symmetry is a choice for THIS map, not a rule — asymmetric maps are expected in map packs.
