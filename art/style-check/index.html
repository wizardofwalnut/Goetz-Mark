Aldermarch Style Check

Does the whole game look like it came from one hand? Checked Oct 30 on 33 screens captured at your S10e's size and all 160 art files measured one by one (colour, brightness, saturation, sharpness, size, background), judged against the Art Bible's target look.

Verdict: two styles, not one. The painted world is coherent: the map, the labor screen, the field pop-up, the resource icons and the troop pictures all share the same warm, chunky 3D-diorama look. The game's own interface around it is a second, flatter "phone app" style: emoji for icons, a cold bright blue for numbers and + buttons, and three different fonts.

Most of the gap is fixable in code (mine) plus one more round of painted icons (ChatGPT). Very little of the painted art needs redoing.

The two styles

Painted world: one style

Same light, camera angle and chunky slabs everywhere. The field pop-up shows how panels can look: painted art inside the dark frame, no emoji.

Interface: a second style

Emoji icons (😊 💰 🌾 ❤️ 🍺 ⚠️), bright blue boxed numbers, loud yellow text boxes, and no painted art at all, even where it exists (the castle panel has no castle picture).

Every screen, rated

Also checked: Save and Load, Event History, Settings, Diplomacy, Caravan, attack prompt. They're plain but quiet (slightly off): the same fonts and emoji headers as the reports.
